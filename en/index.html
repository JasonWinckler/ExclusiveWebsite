GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you may be wondering.

How does the service discount work?

Premium Shadow and VIP Shadow receive 15% off all services offered by Jason Shadow. Mention your active membership when requesting a quote. The service, date and discounted total are agreed before booking.

What is the Exclusive Membership?

Your membership supports Jason Shadow’s music and voice business. Every paid tier includes the same music behind-the-scenes content. Order of Shadows is the name of the community.

Does my membership renew automatically?

No. You choose a fixed term and make a one-time SEPA bank transfer. You decide whether to purchase another term.

What does the VIP meet-up include?

During each active VIP term you can request a personal meet-and-greet by arrangement. Date, place, duration and any travel costs are agreed together in advance. Full conditions are linked in the Legal Center.

Will I automatically receive a newsletter?

No. The newsletter is optional and is sent only after you confirm your consent. Unsubscribe at any time using the email link or your account settings.

Is my contribution a tax-deductible donation?

A membership is paid support for a music and voice business with the stated benefits. It is not offered as a tax-deductible charitable donation.

Is membership an event booking?

No. Your membership supports my work and includes the stated benefits. Music and voice services for your event are requested and agreed separately.
