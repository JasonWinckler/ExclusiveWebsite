# LEGAL REVIEW CHECKLIST

The productive architecture uses Appwrite authentication, Cloudflare Workers,
D1 and private R2 storage. This checklist records the remaining professional
review; it must not be read as a legal approval.

No real customer data, identity documents, challenge videos, bank details,
secrets, databases or backups may be committed to Git. Protected content must
remain fail closed unless account, email, age status, entitlement and device
checks all succeed.

## Launch-blocking checklist
- [ ] Age-verification procedure reviewed.
- [ ] Challenge-video procedure reviewed.
- [ ] Closed user group concept reviewed.
- [ ] Personal second-factor/step-up authentication reviewed.
- [x] Data protection impact assessment technically documented.
- [ ] Data protection impact assessment reviewed by qualified counsel/privacy professional.
- [ ] ID-copy minimization and redaction reviewed.
- [ ] Deletion and key-destruction process reviewed.
- [ ] Impressum reviewed.
- [ ] Privacy policy reviewed.
- [ ] Terms reviewed.
- [ ] Withdrawal flow reviewed.
- [ ] Tax/VAT reviewed.
- [ ] Allowed countries reviewed.
- [ ] Hosting provider reviewed.
- [ ] Youth-protection obligations reviewed.
