LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Legal Notices, Terms and Compliance Disclosures

These template texts are placeholders for professional review. They do not create legal approval, KJM certification, or production authorization.

Terms of Service

Access is restricted to adults who complete registration, email confirmation, manual age verification, step-up authentication and any paid-access requirements. Registration, AVS, payments and adult content are disabled until reviewed. Users must not upload unlawful content, share credentials, bypass access controls, scrape media, or misrepresent age or identity.

Privacy Notice

Data processing must follow minimization, purpose limitation, encrypted temporary AVS storage, short retention and deletion after approval, rejection, withdrawal or expiry. No analytics, ad pixels or unnecessary cookies are used. Production use requires GDPR/DSGVO documentation, a lawful basis, processor contracts and a data-subject request workflow.

Consumer Information

Paid access is planned as prepaid 30, 90 or 365 day access by manual SEPA transfer only. The operator, total price, term, payment instructions, access start, technical requirements, complaint contact and digital-content restrictions must be shown before ordering. Pending accounts cannot pay.

Withdrawal Notice

German/EU consumer-distance rules generally require clear withdrawal information and a model withdrawal route before contract conclusion. Digital content access must not start before the legally required consumer consent and acknowledgement are implemented. The exact wording must be generated or approved by counsel.

Electronic Withdrawal Function

A visible withdrawal function is reserved here for German/EU compliance. It must collect contract identification, consumer contact details and withdrawal confirmation, then issue durable confirmation. It is disabled until the backend exists.

Youth Protection / Age Verification

Adult content must be fully unavailable to minors and unverified users. A simple 18+ button, birthday entry, payment card or email registration is not treated as age verification. Germany-specific age-assurance suitability and legal review must be completed before production.

Content Guidelines

Only lawful, consensual adult content involving adults may be published after review. Performer age and rights records must be maintained outside Git and outside public storage. Unlawful, non-consensual, exploitative, underage, bestiality, violence or otherwise prohibited content is banned.

AI Editing Notice

Promotional profile and banner images may be digitally or partly AI enhanced and are labeled as such. Protected adult content must be real or accurately labeled and must not be misrepresented.

US-Law Oriented Notices

For US availability, the site must account for 18 U.S.C. §2257/§2257A performer recordkeeping where applicable, federal and state age-verification rules, FTC unfair/deceptive-practices standards, clear pricing/refund disclosures and privacy/security obligations. State law can vary and may require geoblocking or additional controls.

German-Law Oriented Notices

For Germany, the site must provide Impressum/provider identification, GDPR/DSGVO privacy information, terms, consumer and withdrawal notices, youth-protection information, reviewed adult closed-group access, no misleading official-approval claims and a legally reviewed AVS process.
